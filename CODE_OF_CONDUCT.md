# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free engineering environment for everyone participating in the DRIFT community—whether filing issues, reviewing behaviour contracts, contributing to language bridges and the open verifier, or discussing specification-driven development.

## Expected Behaviour

- Focus technical discussions on observable evidence, reproducible test cases, and clear specification semantics.
- Respect differing engineering backgrounds, target ecosystems, and language communities.
- Offer constructive critique aimed at code, contracts, and architecture rather than individuals.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and collaborate on corrective fixes.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, derogatory language, or trolling in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Publishing private information (such as physical or electronic addresses) without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, obfuscated payloads, or supply-chain backdoors.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional engineering setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to ban contributors for severe or repeated violations.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainer at `rad-ox@users.noreply.github.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
